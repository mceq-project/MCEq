The reference numbers used during the v2 refactor moved to
mceq-maintenance-tools (`goldens/`), which is where they are regenerated.
They were several MB of stored arrays, most of them tied bitwise to the
numpy/BLAS build that produced them, which forced CI to run one job on a
pinned NumPy/SciPy with AVX-512 disabled. `tests/reference/` replaces them
with a small portable comparison of solved lepton spectra, operator sizes
and the tracked-species list; the pinned reference job and its numerical
pins are gone from CI. Helpers the behaviour tests share moved to
`tests/helpers/`.
