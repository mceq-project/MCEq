`set_mod_pprod` modifies every entry of the channel, including the sub-diagonal entries (child bin above the parent bin), at their x = E_child/E_parent > 1. `_gen_mod_matrix` used to zero the lower triangle of the modification matrix, so any production modification deleted the negative bin-average stencil entries of the v2 databases: an identity modification of p→p and n→n raised the nucleon flux at 1000 g/cm² by 7–8 % (1 TeV proton, FLUKA 2025.1 DB). `misc.gen_xmat` returns the full x matrix (#225, #229).
