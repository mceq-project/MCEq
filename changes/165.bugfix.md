Carry low-energy-model-only projectiles through the runtime HE/LE
cross-section blend instead of dropping them. Previously ``cs_db`` built
the blended table from the high-energy model's parent set alone, so the
dedicated per-species columns of the low-energy model (e.g. FLUKA's n,
n̄, K⁰ and hyperon cross sections) were discarded and ``get_cs`` fell
back to the proton column — for antinucleons this misses the
annihilation part entirely (σ_p̄/σ_p ≈ 1.6 at 1 GeV), inflating the
antineutron flux deep in air showers by up to ~35%. LE-only parents now
keep their own cross section below the transition and use the mapped
high-energy equivalent above it; baryon fallbacks (both in the blend
mapping and in ``get_cs``) prefer the p̄ column for antibaryons.
