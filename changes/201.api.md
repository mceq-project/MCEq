The module layout of v1.4.2 is back:

- `MCEqRun` is in `MCEq.core`; the `MCEq.driver` package is gone. Batched solves,
  `MCEqBatchResult` and `build_phi0_with_cutoff` are in `MCEq.batch`.
- The species are in `MCEq.particlemanager` (the `MCEq.species` package is gone),
  the bin-averaged rate helpers in `MCEq.bin_average`, the initial-state helpers in
  `MCEq.initial_state`.
- The atmospheres are in `MCEq.geometry` under their v1.4.2 module names, NRLMSIS 2.1
  in `MCEq.geometry.msis21`, the target in `MCEq.geometry.target`, the geomagnetic
  cutoff in `MCEq.geometry.cutoff`. All MSIS atmospheres share `MSISAtmosphere` and
  `MSISLocationCentered` with a backend object. `MCEq.core.DENSITY_MODELS` lists the
  keys of `set_density_model`. `AIRSAtmosphere` takes its tables from `table_path`.
- The Data-Driven Model is in `MCEq.ddm` and `MCEq.ddm_utils` (the `MCEq.models`
  package is gone). `DDMSplineDB` is removed: `DataDrivenModel.channels` maps
  `(projectile, secondary)` to the spline entries and `DataDrivenModel.get_entry`
  reads one.
- `MCEq.data` exports the v1.4.2 names `HDF5Backend`, `Interactions`, `Decays`,
  `InteractionCrossSections`, `ContinuousLosses` and `equivalences`; `energy_grid`,
  `gen_xmat` and `normalize_hadronic_model_name` are in `MCEq.misc`.
- One backend dispatch, `MCEq.solvers.make_backend(op, kernel, dtype, device_id,
  operator_dtype)`; backends and `solve_etd2` take the state `dtype`.
  `CudaOperator` is merged into `CudaBackend`; `MCEq.solvers.backends.base` is
  removed, and the operator internals are imported from `MCEq.operators.compiled`.

Removed names:

- `MCEqParticle.is_secondary` and `is_child` (use `sec in proj.hadr_yields` /
  `sec in proj.decay_dists`), `track_decays`, `track_interactions`,
  `init_custom_particle_data`, `set_hadronic_channels`, `set_decay_channels`;
  `ParticleManager.set_cross_sections_db`, `keys`, `nspec`. The `ParticleManager`
  wires the channels; `MCEqParticle` objects compare by identity.
  `MCEqParticle.inel_cross_section` is kept next to `prod_cross_section`.
- `MCEqRun.initial_state` (use `set_single_primary_particle`, `set_initial_spectrum`
  and `get_initial_state`) and the `skip_integration_path` keyword of `solve`.
- `MCEq.misc.theta_deg`, `is_charm_pdgid`, `getAZN_corsika`, `corsikaid2pdg`,
  `pdg2corsikaid`, `reset_plt`, `put_ticks`.
- The `"biased"` and `"centered"` loss stencils, and the Legendre helpers `moments`,
  `forward_weights` and `hemisphere_projection`.

The geomagnetic cutoff of `solve_fullsky` is off by default; pass
`geomagnetic_cutoff=True` to `MCEqRun` or `solve_fullsky`.
