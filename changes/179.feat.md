The sec(theta) transport correction now applies to the batched entry points `solve_batch`, `solve_multirhs` and `solve_fullsky` (numpy, MKL fp64 and CUDA backends): the secant ETD2 driver is generalized to `(dim, K)` states with shared-path (multi-RHS) and per-lane (LPT carousel) step rows, sharing one constant, disk-cached operator set across all columns. Unsupported configurations (accelerate, fp32 outside cuda, EM rho-stack) keep the previous warn ("auto") / raise ("require") behaviour.
