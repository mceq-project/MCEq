Single primaries and spectrum inspection on stencil-averaged tables. `set_single_primary_particle` deposits a particle whose tables the database marks as stencil-averaged on 2r + 1 bins whose reconstructed density has the log-energy moments of a delta at the primary energy (`MCEq.species.bin_average.delta_deposition`); the three-bin deposition, which matches E^0..E^2 at the bin centres, would make the first generation near the primary energy ~5 % low. `MCEqParticle.parent_column` gives the secondaries of a parent at a bin centre the same way; `dN_dxlab`, `dNdec_dxlab`, `dN_dEkin` and `z_factor(definition="primary_e")` use it (to the stencil order: a few per cent at x -> 1 for kernels with sharp kinematic edges, below a per cent elsewhere). Bin-centre databases are unchanged.
