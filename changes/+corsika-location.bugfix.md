`CorsikaAtmosphere` and `IsothermalAtmosphere` keep the `location` and `season`
they were built with; the base constructor used to reset both to `None`. The
CORSIKA parameter sets of one site resolve to it for the geomagnetic cutoff:
SouthPole, PL_SouthPole and SDR_SouthPole to the South Pole, Karlsruhe,
ANTARES/KM3NeT-ORCA to ORCA and KM3NeT-ARCA to ARCA.
