Photons and electrons are no longer carried by the solver unless
`config.enable_em` is set. They have no transport without the
electromagnetic interactions, so `adv_set['disabled_particles']` no longer
needs to list them and now defaults to `[]`; pi0, whose only decay is into
photons, is left out with them. Lepton fluxes are unchanged (agreement to
~1e-7 on the CI database) while the state vector loses two species. The 2D regression fixture
(`tests/data/2d_baseline_solution.npz`) was regenerated for the smaller
state vector: its lepton readouts move by at most 3.8e-5 relative (nu_e),
6e-7 for muons and nu_mu.
