(generated by `scripts/gen_migration_doc.py` from `GROUPS` and the shim
inventory) lists every flat setting name with its group and every moved
import path with its v2 home and 2.0 fate. `docs/api-reference/` gained
pages for `driver`, `environment`, `species`, and `models`; the
`geometry` subtree and the `particlemanager` page are gone (the
environments are documented at `MCEq.environment.*`). The tutorial uses
`set_zenith_azimuth` (D7) and points at the new module paths; its
`densities` reference resolves to the environment page. Deprecation
policy as decided: flat config names stay silent and canonical
forever — no warning machinery was added; only genuinely retired names
warn (`set_theta_deg`, `solve_multirhs`).
