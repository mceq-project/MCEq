`set_single_primary_particle` with the particle in the outermost bin of the energy grid no longer writes into the neighbouring species: the three-bin deposition sliced from bin -1 there, which put part of the particle into the top bin of the species before it in the state vector. The particle is now deposited whole into its bin. Under stencil-averaged tables, primaries within two bins of the grid edge use the three-bin deposition instead of raising.
