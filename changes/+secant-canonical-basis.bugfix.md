2D solutions no longer depend on the BLAS thread count. The sec(theta) coupling block has a degenerate eigenvalue cluster at `1/cos(theta_cap)` (33 of 65 modes at the default 75 deg cap), whose eigenvectors LAPACK chooses arbitrarily and differently per thread count; the choice decides how the muon multiple-scattering damping is split between the exactly integrated diagonal and the explicit remainder, and moved 2D muon spectra by up to 1.8 % (8 vs 16 threads, 100 TeV proton). Every eigenvalue cluster, conjugate pair included, now gets a canonical basis: the invariant subspace from an ordered real Schur form, rotated to diagonalise its projection of `diag(l(l+1))`. The basis is identical to round-off for any thread count and better conditioned (cond(V) 17 instead of 42-292 at 75 deg).
