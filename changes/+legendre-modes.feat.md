2D databases carry the angular distribution as Legendre moments ⟨P_l(cos θ)⟩ on a grid of degrees l (dense from 0, sparse up to 2000), declared by the `common` attribute `angular_basis = "legendre"`. Production, decay and scattering stay diagonal in l on the sphere (Funk–Hecke); the sec(θ) path elongation is the exact coupling M[g] of `MCEq.operators.legendre.path_elongation`, built in seconds; secondaries produced into the backward hemisphere are removed (`config.legendre_backward_removal`); `convert_to_theta_space` interpolates the moments in l and sums the Legendre series. The planar Hankel modes are the small-angle limit, κ = l + ½. A 2D CI database (`mceq_ci_base_air_2d_legendre_v2.h5`, 31 bins × 16 modes) lets the 2D tests run without the production package.
