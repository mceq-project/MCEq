Bin-averaged rates for stencil-averaged databases. Tables built by mceq-maintenance-tools' `bin_average` step act on the parent count density inside each energy bin, reconstructed by a fixed 5-cell quartic stencil (no spectral index; exact to fourth order for any parent spectrum). For the parents such a database marks (`bin_average_parents` on the decay group, `bin_average_projectiles` on a yield table), the decay and interaction rates become the same stencil applied to the rate, a banded operator, in place of `diag(rate)`, so the counted decays and interactions are bin integrals. `physics.bin_average_rates` (default on) switches it; databases without the marks are unchanged.

The v2.0.0 packages listed in `mceq_db_manifest_v2.yaml` (1D base and extra models, 2D Legendre base and SIBYLL23E) are rebuilt from stencil-averaged tables and carry the marks; inclusive conventional muon fluxes drop by about 4 %, muon neutrinos by 2-3 %.

The CI databases (`ci-assets`: the 31-bin 1D packages and the 16-mode 2D Legendre file) are re-cut from the same stencil-averaged databases, and the stored references move with them (`tests/reference/data/reference_solutions.npz`, the 2D baseline solution). The CI files are now published under digest-stamped names that the CI manifest and the workflows name, so an older commit keeps downloading the files it was tested on.
