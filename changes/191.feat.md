Added the longitudinal-profile observables `depth_profile`, `xmax`, `muon_depth_profile` and `muon_xmax` to `MCEqRun`. They read the intermediate solutions of a `solve(int_grid=...)` call and return the particle number, or with `definition="production"` the production rate $dN/dX$ (the source term of the cascade equation), along the depth grid for a chosen energy range and parent category (`total_`, `conv_`, `pr_`, `pi_`, `k_`), together with the depth of the maximum from a Gaisser-Hillas fit (`gaisser_hillas`, `fit_gaisser_hillas`); `muon_xmax` gives the muon $X_{max}$. The new example notebook `Muon_depth_profiles` shows their use, including the muon production depth of a single $10^{19}$ eV shower.
