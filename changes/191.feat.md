Added the longitudinal-profile observables `depth_profile`, `xmax`, `muon_depth_profile` and `muon_xmax` to `MCEqRun`. They read the intermediate solutions of a `solve(int_grid=...)` call and return, along the depth grid and for a chosen energy range and parent category (`total_`, `conv_`, `pr_`, `pi_`, `k_`), the production rate $dN/dX$ (the source term of the cascade equation; default) or with `definition="number"` the particle number, together with the depth of the maximum: a Gaisser-Hillas fit with $X_0 = -45$ g/cm² for the production profile, as in the Pierre Auger Observatory's muon production depth analysis (`gaisser_hillas`, `fit_gaisser_hillas`), a parabola through the grid maximum for the number profile. `muon_xmax` gives the muon $X^\mu_{max}$. The new example notebook `Muon_depth_profiles` shows their use, including the muon production depth of a single $10^{19}$ eV shower.
