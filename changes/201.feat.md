Geomagnetic cutoff maps for the named sites ship with MCEq: downgoing protons on
a 5° zenith and azimuth grid, IGRF at `MCEq.geometry.cutoff.CUTOFF_DATE`
(2025-01-01), made by `scripts/make_cutoff_maps.py`. The CORSIKA parameter sets of
one site and `MSISAtmosphere` use them and reject upgoing directions;
detector-centred MSIS atmospheres trace the sky with gtracr at `CUTOFF_DATE`.
Without an `azimuth_grid`, `solve_fullsky` averages the transmitted primary flux
over azimuth. gtracr is a dependency.
