Requires gtracr 3. The shipped cutoff maps of the named sites were made with gtracr 2, whose azimuth 0 meant "from South" although the maps declare 0 = North, so North and South were swapped (East/West were correct); they were also traced from 100 km above the site, not from where the line of sight enters the atmosphere (up to ~1100 km away near the horizon). The maps are regenerated with gtracr 3 from the entry points and hold the penumbra-resolved effective cutoff R_eff instead of the lowest escaping rigidity. Traced cutoffs are re-cached: the cache key holds the gtracr version.
