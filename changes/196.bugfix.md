`config.cuda_fp_precision = 32` had no effect: `solve()` took no precision and always ran in fp64, and only `solve_batch(..., dtype=np.float32)` reached fp32. `solve`, `solve_from_integration_path`, `solve_batch` and `solve_fullsky` now take `dtype=None`, which resolves to float32 on CUDA when `cuda_fp_precision` is 32 and to float64 otherwise; `solve()` still returns float64 arrays.
