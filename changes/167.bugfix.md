Fixed the atomic-mass unit in ``inverse_interaction_length``: the
conversion used the proton mass instead of m_u, shifting **all**
interaction lengths (and therefore fluxes and shower profiles) by
~0.7%. Numbers produced with earlier versions differ at that level.

Fixed a crash in ``solve()`` for systems whose decay block is empty
(e.g. pure-EM cascades): the BSR kernel builder now zero-pads an empty
``dec_off`` to the correct dimension instead of producing a
shape-mismatched matrix.

The adaptive EM step cap now computes the spectral radius of the EM
block with a dense eigenvalue solve instead of a sparse/norm fallback:
ARPACK does not converge on this highly non-normal block and the matrix
norm overestimates the radius, which made ``enable_em`` runs take far
more steps than necessary (together with the recalibrated
``em_step_safety`` this reduces EM step counts by roughly 3x with
Xmax changes below 0.03 g/cm^2).
