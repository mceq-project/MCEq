The 1D release packages are cut from the v2 1D database with decay tables
filled down to 1 MeV: Pythia decays generated on the full 150-bin grid, and
the helicity-dependent pion, kaon and muon decays computed with exact
kinematics instead of their ultrarelativistic limit. Lepton fluxes above
0.1 GeV move by at most a few per mille; below 0.1 GeV the neutrino spectra
take the decay-at-rest shape. Polarized muon decay now feeds electrons with
the right normalisation when the electromagnetic cascade is enabled.
