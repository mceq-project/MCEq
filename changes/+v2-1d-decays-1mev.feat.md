The v2 data packages carry decay tables filled down to 1 MeV: Pythia decays
generated on the full 150-bin grid, and the helicity-dependent pion, kaon and
muon decays computed with exact kinematics instead of their ultrarelativistic
limit. The hadronic yields of the 1D and 2D packages are re-compacted with
these tables, so resonance and neutral-kaon products below 10 MeV are kept
and e+- appear as secondaries; the 2D decay envelopes of the polarized
channels are exact as well. Lepton fluxes above 0.1 GeV move by at most a few
per mille; below 0.1 GeV the neutrino spectra take the decay-at-rest shape.
Polarized muon decay now feeds electrons with the right normalisation when
the electromagnetic cascade is enabled.
