The v2 data packages carry decay tables down to 1 MeV: Pythia decays on the
full 150-bin grid, and helicity-dependent pion, kaon and muon decays with exact
kinematics instead of the ultrarelativistic limit. The 1D and 2D yields are
re-compacted with these tables, which keeps resonance and K0 decay products
below 10 MeV. Lepton fluxes above 0.1 GeV change by at most a few per mille;
below 0.1 GeV the neutrino spectra follow the decay-at-rest shape. With the
electromagnetic cascade enabled, polarized muon decay feeds electrons with the
correct normalisation.
