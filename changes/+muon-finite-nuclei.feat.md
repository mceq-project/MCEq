The screened Coulomb muon scattering model treats the target as in Geant4's WentzelVI cross section: the nuclear term carries the form factor `(1 + b(1 - cos χ))^-2` with `b = 6.937e-6 MeV^-2 A^0.54 p^2`, and scattering off atomic electrons stops at the kinematic limit `1 - cos χ = m_e T_max / p^2`. Point nuclei over-populated angles beyond ~20° for muons above ~1 GeV (2 GeV, 20–30° at ground: CORSIKA 7 / MCEq 0.87 → 1.00). `config.muon_scattering_finite_nuclei = False` restores point nuclei.
