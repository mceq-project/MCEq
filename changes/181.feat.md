Added :class:`~MCEq.geometry.density_profiles.TabulatedAtmosphere`, which runs
MCEq on a measured or reanalysed atmosphere read from a CSV table, and
:class:`~MCEq.geometry.density_profiles.TabulatedLocationCentered`, which binds
such a table to a detector position.  Both are registered as the
``"Tabulated"`` and ``"Tabulated_LC"`` density models in
:meth:`~MCEq.core.MCEqRun.set_density_model`.

A table gives ``h_cm`` plus either ``rho_gcm3`` or ``T_K`` and ``p_hPa``, and
:meth:`~MCEq.geometry.density_profiles.AtmosphereTable.load_from_csv` reads it,
gzipped or not.  Comment lines, arbitrary column order, unknown columns and
missing values are all accepted.

Optional ``lat_deg``/``lon_deg`` columns make the table a longitude/latitude
grid of columns, which ``TabulatedLocationCentered`` samples at the shower
impact point.  The azimuth angle is then resolved, and a global grid covers
upgoing zenith angles up to 180°, where the shower developed on the far side of
the Earth.  A grid need not be global: a regional one raises when asked for a
position it does not cover, rather than returning a column from somewhere else,
and it interpolates across the 360°/0° seam only when its longitude axis
actually closes on itself.

Profiles are completed to the top of the atmosphere with an isothermal tail
fitted to their two topmost rows (the default, and the only option that needs
no extra information), or by blending into MSIS -- ``top_extension="msis21"``,
``"msis00"``, or ``"msis"`` for NRLMSIS 2.1 when the optional ``nrlmsis``
package is installed and MSISE-00 when it is not.  MSIS is parametrised per
site, so a blend needs either a ``coord`` or one of its named locations; given
a ``coord`` the extension is placed at the column's own position.
``get_pressure`` joins ``get_density`` and ``get_temperature``.
