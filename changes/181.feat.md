Added :class:`~MCEq.geometry.density_profiles.TabulatedAtmosphere`, which runs
MCEq on a measured or reanalysed atmosphere read from a CSV table, and
:class:`~MCEq.geometry.density_profiles.TabulatedLocationCentered`, which binds
such a table to a detector position.  Both are registered as the
``"Tabulated"`` and ``"Tabulated_LC"`` density models in
:meth:`~MCEq.core.MCEqRun.set_density_model`.
Profiles are completed to the top of the atmosphere with an isothermal tail
fitted to their two topmost rows (the default, and the only option that needs
no extra information), or by blending into MSIS -- ``top_extension="msis21"``,
``"msis00"``, or ``"msis"`` for NRLMSIS 2.1.

Gridded tables may now be **ragged**: a column whose lowest levels lie below
the ground -- under the terrain, or under the sea surface wherever the surface
pressure falls below the deepest level -- simply leaves them out, instead of
being padded with invented values to keep the grid rectangular.  Such a table
is aligned by pressure, so it has to carry ``p_hPa``, and levels may be absent
only at the bottom of a column: a gap higher up is missing data rather than
terrain and is still refused.  :meth:`~MCEq.geometry.density_profiles.AtmosphereTable.column`
interpolates from the lowest level all four surrounding nodes share, and
``TabulatedAtmosphere`` continues the column from there down to the observation
level as before.
