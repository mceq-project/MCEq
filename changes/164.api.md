Corrected the Earth radius ``config.r_E`` from 6391 km (a long-standing typo)
to 6371.315 km as used in CORSIKA. Slant depths change by at most +0.15% at
the horizon.

Detector-centred atmospheres (``MSIS00LocationCentered`` and subclasses) now
treat the zenith angle consistently as the angle *at the detector*: the
atmospheric column is integrated at the local zenith angle where the shower
axis crosses the surface (impact-parameter conservation,
``sin(theta_s) = r_det/r_surf * sin(theta)``). The correction is negligible
below 80° but reaches ~1.4° at the horizon for IceCube (the previous
uncorrected column overestimated the horizontal slant depth by ~60%). Grazing
angles just below the geometric horizon (90° < theta ≤ 90° + dip) are now
correctly treated as near-side downgoing columns, and truly upgoing showers
anchor the MSIS atmosphere at the far-side surface crossing where the shower
developed (previously the near-side atmosphere was sampled, giving the wrong
hemisphere and seasonal phase).

``MSIS00LocationCentered`` gained a ``surface_elevation_m`` argument.
``MSIS00IceCubeCentered`` uses it to place the detector 1948 m below the
glacier top at 2835 m elevation; downgoing columns end at the glacier surface
(vertical ``max_X`` ≈ 700 g/cm² instead of ~1020 g/cm² to sea level) while
upgoing columns end at sea level on the far side.

Corrected the KM3NeT ARCA site longitude from 15.4°E to 16.1°E
(Capo Passero, 36°16'N 16°06'E).
