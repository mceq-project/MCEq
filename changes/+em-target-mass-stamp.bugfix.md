An EM database whose cross-section table was scaled for another mean target mass than the one MCEq divides it by is now refused when it is loaded. The table attribute ``mceq_target_mass`` must equal :func:`MCEq.misc.average_A_target` of the medium; a table without it is refused as well. EM air tables built for the molecule-weighted air mass (14.6568) ran every EM interaction length 0.78 % short after the air mass changed to the atom-weighted 14.5431, which made photon showers about 0.7 % (4–7 g/cm²) shallower.
