Added the longitudinal-profile observables `depth_profile`, `xmax`, `muon_depth_profile` and `muon_xmax` to `MCEqRun`. They read the intermediate solutions of a `solve(int_grid=...)` call and return the particle number along the depth grid, for a chosen energy range and parent category (`total_`, `conv_`, `pr_`, `pi_`, `k_`), together with the depth of its maximum; `muon_xmax` gives the muon $X_{max}$. The new example notebook `Muon_depth_profiles` shows their use.
