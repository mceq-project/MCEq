- Tracking particles are transported like their species but produce only
  themselves. `set_interaction_model(..., update_particle_list=False)` no longer
  doubles interaction-tracked fluxes (`prres_*` and other `from_interactions`
  channels) or raises on a model change.
- `config.adv_set["disable_interactions_of_unstable"]` keeps the (anti)nucleons as
  the only projectiles; it removed exactly those before.
- The cutoff initial state (`build_phi0_with_cutoff`, `solve_fullsky`) takes the
  mass groups and their `(Z, A)` from the primary model, as `set_primary_model`
  does: iron enters with A = 54 instead of 56, and groups outside a fixed table are
  kept. Nucleon-flux models such as `GlobalSplineFitBeta` raise `ValueError`
  instead of giving an all-zero state; the neutron flux is kept.
- NRLMSISE-00 and NRLMSIS 2.1 behave the same: MSIS21 samples local noon (it
  sampled 12:00 UT), neither clamps the height, both accept day of year 0 to 366
  and raise `ValueError` on invalid input, and `doy` wins over `season`.
- `CorsikaAtmosphere` and `IsothermalAtmosphere` keep their `location` and `season`.
- `solve(int_grid=...)` works after a solve with a grid of another length.
- `z_factor` weights neutron projectiles with the neutron flux, and the default
  `definition="primary_e"` no longer fails its own assertion.
- `set_single_primary_particle` with a hyperon deposits that particle instead of
  an empty state.
- `set_interaction_model(model, particle_list=...)` reloads when the parent list
  changes.
- `DataDrivenModel` reads `DDM_1.0.npy` from `config.data_dir` and honours
  `e_min`/`e_max`.
- The v1.4 location name `"SanGrasso"` is a deprecated alias of `"GranSasso"`.
