Fixes the Sibyll hadronic interaction matrices of the MCEq database.

In the databases of v1.4.0 and v1.4.1 neutron projectiles were simulated as protons: chromo
dispatched the projectile on `kin.p1.is_nucleus`, which is also true for a single nucleon (A = 1),
so both protons and neutrons were passed to Sibyll's nuclear entry point `sibnuc(A=1)` and the beam
codes distinguishing a proton (13) from a neutron (14) were never reached
([chromo#275](https://github.com/impy-project/chromo/pull/275)).

Restoring the isospin asymmetry of the nucleon cascade lowers the muon charge ratio of Sibyll-2.3d
and Sibyll-2.3e by 13 % between 80 GeV and 1e6 GeV, and raises the prompt electron neutrino flux of
Sibyll-2.1 by up to a factor 4 around 1e8 GeV, which brings it back in line with v1.3.1. Results
obtained with Sibyll-2.1, Sibyll-2.3d or Sibyll-2.3e from the previous database should be recomputed.
The corrected database file is downloaded automatically on the first import.
